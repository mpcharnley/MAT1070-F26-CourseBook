    Applications of Functions.

    Whenever we have an application problem, like the ones we’ve solved before, we can often phrase the question in terms of a function.
    🔗

    Example 64.

    Assume that you are trying to build a rectangular garden bed alongside a house. You have 40 feet of fencing to use to put along the three sides of the garden bed (you don’t need to fence the side that is against the house). Write a function for the area of the garden bed in terms of the length of the bed (the side parallel to the house).
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Piecewise Functions.

    It is sometimes the case that the formula of a function changes depending on the input. The most direct example of this is tax brackets.
    🔗
    We have a specific way of writing out a piecewise function to display the appropriate information.
    🔗
    🔗

    Example 65.

    Consider the function \(f(x)\) defined below
    \begin{equation*} f(x) = \begin{cases} 2x+4 \amp x < -2 \\ 3 \amp -2 \leq x \leq 1\\ 4-x \amp x > 1 \text{.}\end{cases} \end{equation*}
    🔗

    (a)

    Evaluate \(f(-4)\text{,}\) \(f(0)\text{,}\) \(f(-2)\text{,}\) \(f(3)\text{,}\) and \(f(5)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    Sketch a graph of \(f(x)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    🔗

    🔗
    🔗
