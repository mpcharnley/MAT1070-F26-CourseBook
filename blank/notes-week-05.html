    Algebra of Functions.

    Suppose you had two functions:
    • \(P(x)\) - The price of the new base-level iPhone in year x, and
      🔗
      🔗
    • \(M(x)\) - The amount of storage space in the new base-level iPhone in year x.
      🔗
      🔗
    If I want to make a new function, \(C(x)\text{,}\) which is the cost per gigabyte of storage in the new iPhone, how would I do that?
    🔗
     
    🔗
    🔗
    So how do we do this? In the only way we really can; by operating on the outputs.
    🔗

    🔗
    🔗

    \(\frac{f(-2)}{g(-2)}\)

    🔗
    🔗
